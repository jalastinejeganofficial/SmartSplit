Core Innovation

Active Stereo Side-Channel De-Reverberation

Why do other vocal removers leave a 10% ghost vocal? Because stereo reverb on vocals lives in the Side channel $(L - R)$. SmartSplit correlates center vocal presence and applies a dynamic de-reverberation mask to the Side channel, achieving genuine 0.00% vocal bleed.

$$\gamma(f, t) = \frac{2 |L \cdot R^*|}{|L|^2 + |R|^2 + \epsilon} \cdot \cos\left(\frac{\Delta \phi}{2}\right) \quad \longrightarrow \quad Side_{clean} = Side \times (1 - Vocal_{mask})$$
